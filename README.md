# Energy Market AU ⚡🇦🇺

## 📌 Project Overview
This project explores the spatial distribution of energy generation and infrastructure in Australia, with a focus on the National Electricity Market (NEM), using SQL and PostGIS. The goal is to combine data analysis, geospatial reasoning, and energy domain knowledge into a portfolio-ready project.

The project is also motivated by a personal experience. After recently moving to Melbourne, I found it stressful and confusing to understand how the energy market in Victoria operates, particularly the roles of retailers, distributors, and how to choose the most suitable energy provider for my household.

As a starting point, this project focuses on the state of Victoria as a pilot case study. The intention is to progressively expand the analysis to other Australian states in future versions. By translating a personal challenge into a data-driven and spatial analysis, this project aims to help newcomers—and residents alike—gain a clearer understanding of the Australian energy market.

## 🎯 Key Questions
- Where is renewable energy generation concentrated in Australia?
- How does the spatial distribution of wind, solar, and hydro compare across states?
- What is the spatial relationship between renewable generation assets and transmission infrastructure?
- Are there identifiable spatial patterns or clusters by energy type?
